Cyber Shorts

Cyber Shorts is a personal project that makes short news videos about IT and cybersecurity. Videos are generated automatically and the owner reviews each one. Approved videos are uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel only, and are sent to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts that the owner reviews and posts manually in the TikTok app. It does not collect data from viewers and it does not run on behalf of other users.
