Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no external assets.

Data the app handles

The app runs on the owner's own server. It stores news story links and titles, generated scripts, rendered videos, and the access tokens needed to upload to the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts. Tokens are kept in a restricted file on that server. No viewer data is collected.

Third-party services

Story text is sent to an AI model provider to write scripts, and b-roll is fetched from Pexels. Uploads go to YouTube and TikTok under the owner's accounts, subject to those platforms' own privacy policies.

Retention

Rendered video files are deleted after a short period. Draft records are kept for the owner's reference.
